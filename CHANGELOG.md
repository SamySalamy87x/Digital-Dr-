## [0.1.0-alpha] - 2026-06-19

### Added
- Initial release-readiness metadata for GitHub and Zenodo preservation.
- Citation metadata through `CITATION.cff`.

### Release scope
This alpha release documents the Digital Dr product direction as an applied AI healthcare-management prototype. It is not a regulated medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, emergency support, or clinical decision automation.
